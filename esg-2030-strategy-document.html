ESG-2030 Strategy Document for a Major Polymer Manufacturer

A major polymer manufacturer needed to present its ESG strategy through 2030 to a company-wide audience, from executive leadership to plant-floor employees. I designed the full document: layout, grid, infographics, and visual direction.

Task

A large-scale polymer and synthetic-materials manufacturer operates dozens of production facilities across the country and employs tens of thousands of people. The company needed to turn its ESG-2030 roadmap into a single coherent document. The goal was to communicate strategic ambition to everyone at once: employees on the shop floor, executive stakeholders, partners, and regulators.

My task was to design a 13-page strategy presentation from scratch: content layout, grid system, typographic hierarchy, infographics, and visual atmosphere.

Process

I started with the content architecture: nine strategic pillars, each with its own sub-goals, UN SDG alignments, and national-priority references. The density demanded a strict grid and a clear hierarchy before any visual decisions were made.

For the infographics, I leaned into the company’s core product. Each pillar became a molecular diagram: individual nodes represent specific targets, and the lines between them show interdependence. The structure of the graphic mirrors the structure of the strategy which in a company whose entire business is the behavior of matter at a molecular scale is less metaphor than honest description.

The backgrounds came from a hybrid process. I sketched abstract organic forms by hand, then used those sketches as input for an AI image generator, prompting it toward material abstraction and depth — something between scientific visualization and the inherent unknowability of what chemistry actually looks like. The future the document is describing operates the same way: real, but not yet visible. The renders became full-bleed backgrounds composited behind each slide, atmospheric without competing with the text.

Result

A 13-page document now in active use as the primary communication vehicle for the company’s sustainability commitments through 2030. The client signed off on the visual direction without major revisions.
