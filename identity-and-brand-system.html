Identity and Brand System for a Clínica Santamaría

Clínica Santamaría is a prestigious multidisciplinary clinic with over 30 years of experience, located in the very heart of Madrid, Spain. I designed the logo, brand identity, and a detailed brand guideline so the brand can be applied consistently without my involvement.

Task

The clinic needed a visual identity refresh: modernise the brand while preserving its religious reference, then build a complete identity system with guidelines covering all touchpoints, from social media to print advertising.

Result

I developed a clean, type-led identity built around a bold wordmark and a refined graphic element that carries the brand’s spiritual reference with precision and restraint. The result is a system that feels contemporary and clinical without sacrificing character. A comprehensive set of brand guidelines was delivered, defining usage across all media.
