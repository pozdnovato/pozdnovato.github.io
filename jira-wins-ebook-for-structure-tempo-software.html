Jira Wins Ebook For Structure / Tempo Software

Structure by Tempo (formerly known as Structure by ALM Works) is a popular project and portfolio management (PPM) application designed for Atlassian’s Jira. Its marketing team built a 5-chapter customer-story ebook as a lead magnet for the product. I designed the book’s layout system and developed the illustrations from scratch to seed a new brand illustration library.

Task

The marketing team was preparing a series of client-story ebooks titled “Jira Wins: 5 Project Challenges, Solved by Someone Like You”. The series featured five real customer stories about how companies tackled project management challenges using Structure for Jira.

I was responsible for designing the layout system for the entire book and developing the illustrations.

Process

I started by building a modular grid and a set of reusable layout patterns. The chosen approach used a narrow text column with wide margins reserved for illustrations.

For the illustrations, I began with hand-drawn sketches, aligned on the concept with colleagues and the stakeholder, and only moved to final vector execution after the idea was approved. This kept revision cycles short and avoided rework at the polished stage.

Result

The project produced two reusable systems. The first is a layout template that subsequent ebooks in the series could adopt without starting from scratch. The second is an illustration library that went on to be used across the product landing page and email campaigns. Both systems reduced production time for future content and kept the visual style consistent across the Structure / Tempo brand.
