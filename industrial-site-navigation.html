Industrial Site Navigation

A major petrochemical plant operates across a sprawling industrial site: production units, pipelines, warehouses, and restricted zones. Its existing site scheme tried to show everything at once: dense, detailed, and hard to read for the employees who navigate the territory every day on foot and by company transport.

I rebuilt it as a reduced navigation system: only the main roads, key production zones, and approved transport routes, unified into one visual language that reads at a glance and holds up as a working map.

Task

A major petrochemical plant producing polyethylene and polymer materials spans a vast, complex industrial site. Employees move across production units, pipelines, warehouses, labs, and restricted zones daily, on foot and by company transport running on approved routes.

Clear navigation matters here: it helps people find destinations fast, avoid hazardous zones, and save time during shifts, maintenance, and emergencies.

Main task was to simplify the site scheme to the essentials: main roads and key production areas. And the second layer: to add the facility’s internal transport routes.

Process

I stripped the scheme to its functional core, keeping only what people use to orient themselves: major roads, labeled production zones, and approved transport routes.

Result

The scheme is easy to read and far more functional for everyday use. Employee feedback has been positive: orienting across the site got noticeably easier.
